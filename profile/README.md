# KiloKilo Games

**An independent game studio, started by two friends in a dorm room.**

We make games we'd want to play ourselves: focused, polished, and built with care. What began as late-night prototyping between lectures is now a studio with a simple goal: to ship fun, memorable experiences.

---

## What We Do

- **Game development**: designing and building original indie titles
- **Tools & tech**: developing the internal tools and libraries we use along the way
- **Open source**: sharing code, assets, and learnings with the community where we can

## Our Values

- **Play first.** If it isn't fun, it doesn't ship.
- **Small team, high standards.** We stay lean and put quality ahead of scope.
- **Build in the open.** We share our process and welcome community feedback.
- **Keep learning.** Every project makes the next one better.
